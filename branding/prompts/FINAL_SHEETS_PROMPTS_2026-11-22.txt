GLOBAL RULES (paste at the start of EVERY prompt)
==================================================
Same character as the attached reference sheet, do not redesign. Flat-colour cartoon sticker style, thick dark-green outline (same line weight in every cell), glossy green skin, light-green belly scales. SMALL dragon: short, WIDE, chubby body, big hood, exactly TWO small hands (the body's own, never extra arms), tail curled. WISE dragon: same snake body type, straw conical hat, long white beard and white eyebrows, wooden cane. Pure white background (#FFFFFF), no ground shadow, no text, no numbers, no labels, no cell borders. All cells exactly the same size, character centred, the BASELINE (where the body touches the ground) on the same horizontal line in every cell, and the SAME SCALE in all cells (no zoom between frames). Effects must not touch the character unless stated. Read left to right, top to bottom = animation order.

SHEET G - wise dragon slither walk (4 columns x 2 rows = 8 cells, loop)
==================================================
SHEET G, 4 columns x 2 rows, 8 cells. Wise dragon, pure SIDE view facing LEFT (profile). Right hand holds the cane ahead of him, slightly angled forward. He moves by slithering: the S-shaped wave travels from head to tail, advancing 45 degrees of wave phase per frame, so frame 9 would equal frame 1.
Frame 1: wave phase 0, cane tip touching the ground (tap). Frame 2: phase 45, cane lifting 10 degrees. 3: phase 90, cane in the air. 4: phase 135, cane in the air, hat bobs 2% down. 5: phase 180, cane tip touches the ground again (tap). 6: phase 225. 7: phase 270. 8: phase 315.
Tail tip swings the opposite way to the head every frame. Beard sways opposite to the direction of motion with a small delay. Head height changes at most 5% between frames. Hat bobs very slightly. Calm stern face, eyes open, mouth closed in all frames. Body length and thickness stay constant (no stretching).
