=====================================================================
GLOBAL RULES (paste at the start of EVERY prompt)
=====================================================================
Same character as the attached reference, do not redesign. Flat-colour cartoon sticker style, thick dark-green outline (same line weight in every cell and on every prop/garment), glossy green skin, light-green belly scales, hood with the two soft spiral marks on the inner sides, exactly as in the reference.
HANDS RULE: exactly TWO hands, the body's own (small ones on short arms coming from the upper torso under the hood). Never extra hands, never hands growing from the head or hood, never a third hand holding a prop.
BODY RULE: do NOT invent a new snake body. Copy the body, the belly-scale pattern, the coil and the tail from the reference. SMALL dragon = short, WIDE, chubby body with a big hood and a curled tail (never a tall thin neck). BIG dragon = same face and hood design but taller: a longer upright neck (about 1.5x the small one), slimmer chubby body, the same cartoon proportions.
Props and clothes are part of the character: same dark outline, same flat shading, fully CLOSED outline (no gaps), so a white garment is still enclosed by a dark line. Never fill a garment with pure #FFFFFF: use a very light cool grey (#E6ECF2) for white things with slightly darker fold lines.
Pure white background (#FFFFFF), no ground shadow, no text, no letters, no Arabic calligraphy, no numbers, no labels, no cell borders, no frames, no props on the floor unless a frame says so.
GEOMETRY (important, the sheets are cut by a script):
 - SMALL dragon cells are 512 x 512 px. Character height about 400 px max (cap/crown included), centred horizontally at x = 256, BASELINE (where the body touches the ground) at y = 450 in every cell.
 - BIG dragon cells are 512 x 640 px, character height about 540 px, centred at x = 256, baseline at y = 590.
 - Sheet size = columns x 512 by rows x 512 (4x2 = 2048x1024, 3x2 = 1536x1024, 4x3 = 2048x1536, 4x1 = 2048x512). BIG sheets use 640 for the cell height. If the tool cannot output exact pixels, keep the exact aspect ratio and the exact grid; cells must be equal and nothing may cross a cell edge.
 - SAME SCALE in all cells of a sheet (no zoom between frames). Jumps lift the body above the baseline; the baseline line itself never moves.
 - Effects (stars, dust, steam, confetti, sparkles, zzz) are NOT drawn on character sheets. They live only in sheet SX. Do not draw them unless a frame says so.
 - Reading order, left to right then top to bottom = animation order.
Angles used below: 0 deg = front, 90 deg = side profile, 180 deg = back. "Turned to his left" = the viewer sees the character's left side.
